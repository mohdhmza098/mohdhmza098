### A Little About Me

I'm currently pursuing my B.Tech in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at Integral University, Lucknow. I enjoy exploring technology, understanding how things work behind the scenes, and turning what I learn into something practical.

My journey has taken me through different areas of tech, from working with data and machine learning to exploring frontend and backend development. Right now, I'm especially interested in Java, web development, and building applications that are useful in the real world.
